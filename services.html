Our 10 Specialized Services

Comprehensive ground travel, protocol, security escort, route analysis, driver outsourcing, and 24/7 emergency response capabilities across Nigeria and 8 African countries.

1. Meet-and-Greet / Airport Protocol

We provide meet-and-greet and airport protocol services to our clients. Special and detailed Country Briefings are given to new arrivals in Nigeria at the airport, including passenger vehicle(s) and escort team(s) as required.

Request Airport Protocol

2. Dedicated Services

Conveyance and escort for visitors from the airport to hotels, various offices, or private residences, carried out by highly trained DDC (Defensive Drivers’ Course) and EMT (Evasive and Maneuvering Techniques) Security Drivers and Escort Commanders trained in Executive Protection. Vehicles are equipped with Real-Time Vehicle Trackers and radios.

Request Dedicated Service

3. Event Security

We assist in providing customer-friendly event security and crowd management services. Whether for a social or corporate event of any size, our security officers are fully trained and prepared to meet any requirement your function presents.

Request Event Security

4. Close / Executive Protection

We make available on request professional bodyguards (CP) well trained in Martial Arts. Our CP teams complete the Close Protection Course and receive continuous training to keep their expertise cutting-edge with strong diplomatic skills.

Request Close Protection

5. Escort Service

We provide dedicated escort services to and from any location, enhancing security across urban routes, business districts, and industrial sites with our field expertise.

Request Escort Service

6. Interstate Travels

We provide interstate travel for and with our principals to all desired locations across our Nigerian hubs and regional corridors with luxury, security, and comfort.

Request Interstate Travel

7. Route Analysis

Detailed trip planning providing the quickest, shortest, and most scenic routes, backed by real-time discovery of routed networks, visualization of primary and redundant paths, historical routing visibility, anomaly detection, and multi-protocol routing.

Request Route Analysis

8. Drivers Outsourcing

We provide and outsource duly vetted and professional drivers to client offices, homes, industrial sites, or any other locations requested by the client.

Request Vetted Drivers

9. Emergency / Quick Response Services

Motorized Emergency Response services to attend to kidnaps, robbery attacks, social unrest, and medical emergencies. Our Control Centre operates 24/7 to receive distress calls and alarm system alerts, responding rapidly to minimize threats.

Connect With 24/7 Control Centre

10. Investigation, Due Diligence Checks & Vetting

We carry out standard investigation procedures tailored to client requirements and deliver thorough due diligence checks and personnel vetting for organizations.

Request Vetting & Due Diligence
