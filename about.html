Corporate Profile

Pacesetter Logistics & G-Travels Limited

Pacesetters is a one-stop shop for professional journey management services, armed escort, protocol, route analysis, outsourcing, and events executives, delivered through our professional and experienced teams for the safety and success of our clients’ businesses.

“We take time to listen, understand, and utilize what makes us different and how we set ourselves apart.”
Security and Safety in Mind

Our Vision

To be recognized as the global leader in travel management solutions: an entrepreneurial, innovative, and inspiring company of choice for employees, customers, partners, and shareholders. We go above and beyond in delivering benefit to all stakeholders, living by our corporate values both internally and externally while developing a highly motivated and empowered workforce.

Our Mission

To be travel management leaders in all regions in which we operate, using innovative technology to improve the customer experience and bring positive change to the market. At all times, we demonstrate a return on investment to our customers and suppliers, empowering our teams to make the best decisions for our customers while keeping shareholders at the forefront of our business growth strategy.

Our Commitments

Delivering Value Across Every Stakeholder

Customers

We provide superior and reliable ground travel solutions, continually developed in partnership with our clients to satisfy their ever-changing risks.

Shareholders

We maximize shareholder value by involving all our stakeholders and suppliers in providing focused, market-leading solutions within a framework of continuous improvement and sound financial management.

Society

We benefit society by operating safely, caring for the environment, helping appropriate voluntary organizations in sharing responsibility for our world, and promoting business excellence.
